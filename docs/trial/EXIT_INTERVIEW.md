# Exit interview questions

Asked at the end of day 7, separately, by a team member. About 20 minutes each. The person
may skip any question. Notes are kept by the household or the team member without names.

## Older adult
1. Tell me about a time you talked to it that you enjoyed.
2. Was there a time it annoyed you, or got something wrong?
3. Did it ever remember something about you or your family? How did that feel?
4. Was there anything you did not want it to know or remember?
5. Did you feel it was a person, a machine, or something in between? Does that matter?
6. Would you keep using it? When would you use it, and when would you not?
7. What would you change first?

## Caregiver
1. How much of the week did you spend setting up, fixing or checking on it?
2. Did the panel tell you anything you did not already know? Was it useful or worrying?
3. Walk me through each flag it raised. Which were right, and which were not?
4. Was there anything it should have flagged and did not?
5. Did you change what it remembered? Was that easy?
6. How did you feel about the text going to Nebius, now that you have used it?
7. Did it change how you check in on your relative, for better or worse?
8. Would you want it to contact you or others itself? In what situations?
9. What would have to be true for you to keep using it?
10. Anything we did not ask?

## Closing both
Ask whether they want any data deleted now, and offer to help delete `data/`, the `.env` file
and the daily log, and to revoke the Nebius key.
