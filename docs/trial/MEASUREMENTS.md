# Measurements for the week

Fixed before day 1; we will not add measures after seeing results. Counts only: no names, no
conversation text, nothing from `data/` is copied into the repo.

## What we count each day
1. **Turns**: how many times the older adult spoke to the companion (from the caregiver log
   or the on-screen history).
2. **Safety fast-path flags**, sorted by the caregiver using what they know:
   - **True**: it flagged something that really was a concern.
   - **False**: it flagged something that was not.
   - **Missed**: a concern the caregiver knows about that was not flagged.
3. **Memory corrections used**: times someone fixed or removed a remembered fact (by saying
   "forget ...").
4. **One open question a day** to each participant, asked by the caregiver in the evening:
   - Older adult: "What did you and the companion talk about that you liked or didn't?"
   - Caregiver: "Was there anything today you wish it had done differently?"
   Record the answer in the person's own words, on paper, kept in the household.

## Daily log (two lines, kept by the caregiver)
```
Day _  Turns: __  Flags true/false/missed: __/__/__  Memory corrections: __
Open question answers (one phrase each): ____________________________
```

## What leaves the house
At the end, after the family reads it and agrees, the seven daily number lines (the first
line only) are handed to the team. The answers to the open questions stay with the family
unless they choose to share a sentence.

## Safety misses
Any miss (a concern that was not flagged, or a wrong reply to a worrying statement) is
reported to the team the same day so it can be filed as its own issue, described without
identifying details.
