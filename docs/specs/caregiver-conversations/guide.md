# Caregiver call guide

How to use this: read the consent line first. Spend about 2 minutes on each question. Take notes in the notes template.

> This is for a hackathon entry. Nothing you say is recorded or published with your name. I will summarise what three people told us, in general terms.

1. When you are not with your parent, what do you most want to know, and how do you find out today?
   *Follow-up: What do you do when you cannot find out?*

2. Tell me about the last time something worried you that you learned about late.
   *Follow-up: What would have helped you hear about it sooner?*

3. If a voice companion could be briefed by you, what three things would you tell it first?
   *Follow-up: What is the one thing you would never want it to forget?*

4. What should it never tell you about? What should it never do?
   *Follow-up: Is there anything it should always tell you right away?*

5. We show the parent out loud that the family is being told, before the family hears it. How does that land with you?
   *Follow-up: Is there anything about that you would want done differently?*

6. What would make you stop using it after a week?
   *Follow-up: What would make you keep using it?*

Thank you for your time.
